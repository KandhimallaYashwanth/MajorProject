1. EXECUTIVE AUDIT SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This forensic audit evaluated the empirical claims, statistical procedures, and reviewer responses for Paper #516 ("Adaptive Hybrid Quantum-Classical Neural Network for Early Medical Disease Diagnosis Using Explainable AI") against raw experimental outputs and reviewer feedback (Reviewers #2, #4, #6, #7, #8, #9, #10, and #11).

Key Findings:
1. NUMERICAL INTEGRITY: 100% verified across 300 repeated-CV runs, 25-fold ablations, and statistical test outputs. All values in Table I–V of the revised manuscript strictly match raw CSV data.
2. AMBIGUOUS CASE ACCURACY DELTA: The claim that "quantum escalation actively rescues or recovers ambiguous cases failing preliminary classification" is EMPIRICALLY CONTRADICTED on Pima Diabetes. On Diabetes 5-fold CV, samples routed to Path 1 dropped in accuracy from 56.60% (preliminary) to 54.72% (quantum), and Path 2 dropped from 58.33% to 52.78% (m3_per_path_accuracy.csv). Quantum escalation does not guarantee error correction; it recalibrates global trade-offs.
3. CLASSICAL BOTTLENECK ABLATION: The degradation of the classical 4-D bottleneck (-5.72% F1 on Heart, -4.85% on Diabetes) shows that a simple 4-D dense block suffers representational collapse compared to the 4-qubit VQC in this pipeline. However, this does NOT establish general "quantum-specific advantage" because a 4-qubit circuit has only 16 states and is classically simulable.
4. SIMULATOR TIMING VS HARDWARE: The reported 98x to 155x speedup for Path 0 is strictly a CPU state-vector numerical linear algebra benchmarking artifact (PennyLane default.qubit). It must not be conflated with physical quantum hardware speedup or cryogenic energy reduction.
5. STATISTICAL PARITY FALLACY: Failure to reject the null hypothesis under the Nadeau-Bengio resampled t-test (p >= 0.05) does not prove mathematical equivalence or predictive parity. F1 point estimates for Adaptive HQNN are slightly lower than Fixed HQNN on WDBC (-0.53%) and Diabetes (-0.38%).
6. REVIEWER CONCERNS RESOLUTION FRAMING: Technical and empirical requests (repeated CV, Nadeau-Bengio tests, gate counts, ablations, SHAP stability) were successfully resolved with new experiments. However, intrinsic study boundaries (small cohort scale N=303-768, simulation vs physical QPU hardware, lack of tree-based baselines, and unvalidated clinical readiness) must be documented as persistent study limitations, not claimed as "resolved."
